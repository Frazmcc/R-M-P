HOW RATE MY POO WORKS

Rate My Poo: how to vote and submit

Rate My Poo brings back the playful, no-nonsense spirit of the early internet's photo-rating sites. You can browse approved poo photos, cast a score from 1 to 10 and see which photos earn the highest and lowest ratings. The site is free, ad-free and doesn't require a visitor account.

1. Browse and rate a photo

Choose Rate a Poo to see a published photo. Give it one score from 1 (lowest) to 10 (highest). Each browser receives an anonymous identifier that helps prevent accidentally voting twice for the same photo. Ratings are averaged across recorded votes; determined abuse may still require moderator intervention.

2. Explore the rankings

Visit the Top 20 for the highest-scoring photos, the Bottom 20 for the lowest-scoring, Staff Favourites for moderators' picks, or Latest for recently approved submissions.

3. Submit your own photo

Open Submit Your Poo and provide a title, optional nickname and a JPEG, PNG or WebP photo under 6 MB. You must be at least 18, have the right to publish the image and confirm that it doesn't identify or expose anyone else.

Each submission is converted into a smaller WebP image before storage. The original upload isn't saved by the application. Only the converted photo enters a private moderation queue. After approval, that same WebP becomes publicly visible and eligible for ratings.

4. Keep the site fun and respectful

No harassment, personal information, nudity, identifiable people or illegal material. Readers can report a published photo to the moderators using its Report photo control. Reports are reviewed, and inappropriate content can be removed.
