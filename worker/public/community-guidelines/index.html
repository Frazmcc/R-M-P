COMMUNITY GUIDELINES

Keep Rate My Poo funny, safe and fair

Rate My Poo is an ad-free community for light-hearted photos and 1–10 ratings. Every upload is reviewed before it can appear in the public gallery. These rules help protect other people and the community.

Who can submit?

Submitters must be 18 or over, have created or have the necessary rights to publish their photo, and ensure anyone affected has consented. Do not upload photos containing identifiable people or personal details.

What can I upload?

Original photos of poo that you are entitled to share, with a short, non-abusive title. Accepted formats are JPEG, PNG and WebP up to 6 MB each. An optional public nickname may appear alongside an approved photograph.

What is not allowed?

  • Photos showing identifiable people, private information, nudity, or sexual exploitation.
  • Harassment, threats, discriminatory abuse, scams or misleading impersonation.
  • Copyright infringement or material you do not have permission to publish.
  • Illegal content, violence or images intended to cause harm.
  • Repeated uploads, manipulated ratings or other spam and abuse.

What happens to my image?

Uploads are validated and converted to WebP, with image metadata removed during conversion. The application stores only the converted WebP, not a second copy of your source image. The converted image is private while pending moderator review. Approval makes it visible publicly; rejection or removal clears the live stored image. Cloud providers may retain backups or history temporarily under their own policies.

Reporting and moderation

If a published photo breaks these rules, select Report photo beneath the photo and describe the problem. Moderators can review reports, remove images and reject submissions. We may remove content without notice when needed for community safety.

Privacy

We keep published titles, optional nicknames, rating scores and pseudonymous identifiers to support voting and limit abuse. Do not include sensitive information in submissions. A dedicated contact method and formal privacy and takedown procedures should be published before a large public launch.
